# Surface brief — Explorer (primary), plus Volumes and Scan

## Scope and visitor mode
Operate. One user, at a desktop, hunting what is eating disk space. The treemap is the product; every other region serves it. Three surfaces share one system: Volumes (choose a target), Scan (live progress), Explorer (the treemap drill-down).

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints
Technical desktop user, keyboard-first, judges craft at pixel level. Job: scan a drive, see proportion, drill to the offender. Action: pan, select, zoom in, act on a file. Proof: a treemap that fills its panel exactly, labels that never collide, motion that never blocks. Constraints: read-only; no deletion anywhere; left button carries the primary drag; banned fonts Inter/Roboto/Arial/Open Sans/Helvetica; no gradients, glassmorphism, ambient glows, uniform card grids, blinking dots, or redundant subheadings.

## Chosen direction
User-pinned canon at full fidelity: **Apple system apps** (Finder, Disk Utility, Photos) as the craft bar. Borrow the discipline — neutral slightly-cool surfaces, one family at real weight steps, hairline separators instead of cards, 8pt rhythm, tabular figures, one motion curve — never Apple's chrome, icons, or copy. The roll's costume worlds are superseded by the pin; four named raises from the declined challengers survive into the build.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A disk analyzer whose chrome demotes itself to system furniture so the treemap is the only thing on screen with color and weight. It refuses the category's two defaults: the boxed card grid and the decorative "designed" dashboard with a big number, a small label and an accent.

### OWN-WORLD
Neutral surfaces tinted very slightly cool — never warm cream — in a matched light and dark pair. One sans (Geist) at 400/500/600 and one mono (Geist Mono) for every figure, tabular-nums everywhere a number can change. Hairline 1px separators do all the dividing; panels are defined by fill and edge, never by shadow. 8pt spacing rhythm; radius 10 on panels, 6 on controls. The only saturated color on screen is the treemap's eight-category data palette plus one selection blue; categories carry a label and a pattern as well as a hue, so nothing is encoded by color alone.

### STORY
The user opens on Volumes, scans a target, lands in an Explorer where the treemap fills its panel edge to edge at the panel's exact aspect. Left-drag pans anywhere. Shift-drag rubber-bands a selection. A click selects; a second click on a folder expands that tile to fill the frame while the next level resolves underneath. The rail mirrors every selection, searches, sorts, and offers reveal / copy path / open. Breadcrumbs and back-forward carry the whole history.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
44px title bar: wordmark `space`, screen label, live readout `8.31 GB · 413 files`, appearance toggle, ⌘K hint. 40px path bar: back, forward, breadcrumb `D:\ › DC FRIENDS`, filter field, overflow actions. 52px stat row: four tabular figures (Folder size 40.9 MB, Files 56, Items 9/9, of volume 0.5%) separated by hairlines, no card around them. Main split: treemap filling the left at the container's exact aspect with zero letterboxing, 340px rail right (tabs, nine rows, selection card, footer hint). 36px legend strip of eight category chips with share figures. Signature moment: the shared-element zoom — the clicked folder's tile expands to fill the canvas over 420ms on one curve while the new level fades up beneath it.

## Memorable moment
Clicking a folder and watching that exact rectangle swell to fill the frame — the level change reads as one continuous move, not a cut.

## Unresolved decisions
